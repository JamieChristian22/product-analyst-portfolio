# Executive Summary — User Journey to First Purchase

## Decision Context
The product team wants to shorten time-to-first-purchase and determine which acquisition sources and user behaviors produce the highest-quality purchase journeys.

## Executive KPIs
- Users analyzed: **3,000**
- Average sessions before first purchase: **3.96**
- Average events before first purchase: **25.0**
- Median time to first purchase: **11.02 days**
- Users purchasing within 7 days: **20.6%**
- Average first-purchase revenue: **$152.77**

## Findings
1. **Referral** produces the strongest 7-day purchase rate at **26.5%**.
2. **Social** is the weakest medium at **10.5%**, creating the clearest journey-quality investigation.
3. Sessions before purchase and journey duration have a Spearman correlation of **0.79** (p=0), supporting the use of repeated sessions as a friction signal.
4. Bringing the weakest medium to the portfolio-wide 7-day benchmark is modeled to move about **41 additional users** into a faster first-purchase journey.

## Recommendation
Prioritize onboarding, landing-page/message-match, and return-journey continuity for high-friction acquisition segments. Validate causal impact with a controlled experiment rather than assuming acquisition medium itself causes slower conversion.
