# Stakeholder Readout
## One-line takeaway
Journey friction varies meaningfully by acquisition source; **Social** should be the first diagnostic target.

## Product Actions
- Product: prioritize first-purchase journey continuity.
- Growth: evaluate channels on journey quality, not click volume alone.
- Design: reduce repeated discovery work across sessions.
- Engineering: preserve state/recently viewed/cart where appropriate.
- Analytics: instrument first-touch → first-purchase cohorts and experiment exposure.

## Important Limitation
The dataset is simulated and observational. Segment differences are diagnostic; they do not establish causality.
