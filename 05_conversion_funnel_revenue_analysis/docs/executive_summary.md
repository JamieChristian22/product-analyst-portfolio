# Executive Summary

## Business Question
Where does the conversion funnel lose the most users, which customer segments generate the strongest revenue, and which product changes have the greatest modeled revenue upside?

## Executive KPIs
- Viewers: **5,000**
- Purchases: **2,076**
- Overall conversion: **41.5%**
- Revenue: **$188,215.53**
- AOV: **$90.66**
- Average time to purchase: **89.9 minutes**

## Findings
- The largest absolute funnel loss occurs before **Add to Cart**.
- **Desktop** has the strongest modeled device conversion.
- **Organic** has the strongest modeled channel conversion.
- **US** contributes the most modeled geographic revenue.
- The highest-friction device/channel intersection is **tablet × none**.

## Decision
Prioritize View → Add-to-Cart friction first, then target high-friction device/channel segments. Validate with an A/B test and measure conversion lift, revenue/session, AOV, and performance guardrails.
