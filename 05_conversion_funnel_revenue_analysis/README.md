# 🧠 Product Analytics Mission

This project investigates a critical Product and Growth Analytics question:

> **Where does conversion friction occur, which users are most affected, and how could reducing that friction improve both purchase behavior and revenue?**

The analytical workflow connects:

**👀 Product Exposure → 🛒 Intent → 💳 Checkout → ✅ Purchase → 💰 Revenue**

while also measuring:

**⏱️ Time-to-Purchase • 📱 Device • 📣 Channel • 🌎 Geography**

The objective is not simply to identify which segment has the most revenue.

The goal is to determine:

* Where the customer journey breaks down
* Which segments experience the greatest friction
* Whether slower journeys correspond with weaker business outcomes
* Which product opportunity should be prioritized first
* How improvement should be validated
* What the potential revenue impact could look like

# 📊 Executive KPI Framework

## 👥 Funnel Users

Measure unique users reaching each funnel stage.

Stages include:

**View • Add to Cart • Checkout • Purchase**

---

## 📉 Stage Conversion

Measures movement between consecutive funnel stages.

### Examples

```text
View → Cart Conversion
=
Add-to-Cart Users ÷ Viewers
```

```text
Cart → Checkout Conversion
=
Checkout Users ÷ Add-to-Cart Users
```

```text
Checkout → Purchase Conversion
=
Purchasers ÷ Checkout Users
```

---

## 🏆 Overall Purchase Conversion

```text
Overall Conversion
=
Purchasers ÷ Viewers
```

### Product Question

> **How efficiently does product exposure become completed customer value?**

---

## ⏱️ Time-to-Purchase

Measures elapsed time between the beginning of the analyzed journey and purchase.

### Product Question

> **How much time does the customer need before completing the purchase journey?**

Longer purchase time can reflect:

* Normal consideration
* Product uncertainty
* Navigation friction
* Mobile usability problems
* Checkout friction
* Missing information
* Price hesitation

Therefore:

> **High time-to-purchase is a diagnostic signal, not automatically proof of a problem.**

---

# 💰 Revenue

Measures observed transaction value generated by purchased users.

Revenue is analyzed alongside behavioral metrics so the project does not optimize conversion while ignoring commercial impact.

---

# 🛍️ Average Order Value

```text
AOV
=
Revenue ÷ Purchases
```

### Business Question

> **How much transaction value does the average purchase generate?**

AOV also supports directional opportunity sizing.

# 🔥 Primary Product Insight

![Insight](https://img.shields.io/badge/KEY_INSIGHT-FUNNEL_FRICTION_HAS_REVENUE_COST-DC2626?style=for-the-badge)

The central idea of this project is:

> **Conversion friction should be prioritized not only by percentage loss, but by user volume, segment severity, and potential financial impact.**

A stage with a moderate conversion rate but very high traffic may represent a larger opportunity than a smaller stage with worse percentage conversion.

Therefore, prioritization combines:

**Funnel Loss × Segment Friction × Revenue Exposure × Confidence**

---

# ⏱️ Time-to-Purchase as a Product Signal

![Time](https://img.shields.io/badge/SIGNAL-TIME_TO_PURCHASE-0891B2?style=for-the-badge)

Time-to-purchase provides a different view of friction than conversion alone.

Two segments might have the same purchase rate.

But one may require:

* More time
* More interactions
* More return visits
* More customer effort

That can indicate a weaker experience.

### Product Principle

> **Conversion tells us whether users succeed. Time-to-purchase helps show how difficult success may be.**

---

# 📱 Device Friction

Potential product questions:

* Does mobile require longer to reach purchase?
* Does mobile have weaker stage conversion?
* Does mobile generate healthy revenue despite slower journeys?
* Is tablet worth prioritizing given its traffic and business contribution?

### Decision Principle

Do not prioritize a segment only because it has the worst percentage metric.

Consider:

**Reach • Severity • Revenue Exposure • Confidence • Effort**

---

# 📣 Channel Quality

Channels should be compared across both behavioral and commercial outcomes.

A strong channel should ideally produce users with:

✅ Healthy conversion
✅ Reasonable purchase time
✅ Strong revenue
✅ Healthy AOV

### Product/Growth Principle

> **Faster conversion is not automatically better if customer or revenue quality is weak.**

---

# 🌎 Geographic Analysis

Geographic revenue analysis supports questions such as:

* Which countries produce the most value?
* Which markets have strong conversion?
* Are high-engagement markets under-monetized?

Geographic differences generate hypotheses.

They do not prove the cause of behavior.

**5% funnel-loss recovery**

### 🔵 Target

**15% funnel-loss recovery**

### Framework

```text
Recovered Purchasers
=
Stage Drop-Off × Recovery %
```

```text
Revenue Opportunity
=
Recovered Purchasers × AOV
```

These values provide scale and decision context.

They should not be interpreted as guaranteed financial results.

* user_id

* revenue

### Principle

> **If product behavior is not instrumented consistently, funnel metrics can create false confidence.**
