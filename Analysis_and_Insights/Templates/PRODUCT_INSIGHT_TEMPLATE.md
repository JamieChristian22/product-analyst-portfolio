# Product Insight Template

## Decision
What decision must the team make?

## Observation
What happened?

## Evidence
Which metric/segment/time period supports it?

## Why It Matters
What user or business outcome is affected?

## Root-Cause Hypothesis
Why might this be happening?

## Recommendation
What should change?

## Validation
What experiment or analysis will validate the recommendation?

## Primary Metric
What determines success?

## Guardrails
What must not deteriorate?

## Business Impact
What is the expected or modeled value?

## Final Decision
Ship / iterate / stop / investigate.
