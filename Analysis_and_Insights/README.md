This is the decision layer for the Product Analyst portfolio.

## Product Analyst Standard
The goal is not to produce more charts. The goal is to answer:
1. What happened?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What should the product team do?
4. How should it be prioritized?
5. How will we know the change worked?
6. What business outcome should improve?

## Data / Evidence Transparency
This decision center synthesizes the analytical frameworks and modeled outputs developed across the portfolio projects. It does not present modeled assumptions as real company results.
