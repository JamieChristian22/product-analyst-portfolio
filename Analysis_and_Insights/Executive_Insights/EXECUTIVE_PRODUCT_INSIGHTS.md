# Executive Product Insights

## Purpose
This folder is the portfolio's **Insights → Decision → Action** layer. Projects 01–06 prove the analysis can be performed; this section proves the analysis can be translated into product decisions.

## Portfolio-Level Findings
1. **Funnel friction is repeatedly measurable and actionable.** Projects 01 and 05 identify conversion losses that can be prioritized by volume, segment, and revenue impact.
2. **Activation and journey quality matter before monetization.** Projects 02 and 03 connect first value and journey speed to downstream behavior.
3. **Growth should not be scaled without healthy economics.** Project 04 demonstrates that negative observed-period economics should change the investment decision rather than be hidden.
4. **Internal products deserve product analytics too.** Project 06 treats Salesforce as a workflow product and connects user behavior to pipeline outcomes.

## Executive Principle
A metric is useful only when it changes a decision. Every recommendation in this portfolio is paired with a success metric, guardrail, validation method, and business outcome.
