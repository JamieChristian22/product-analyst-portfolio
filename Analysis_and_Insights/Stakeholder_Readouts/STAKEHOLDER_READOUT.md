# Cross-Functional Stakeholder Readout

## Product
Prioritize customer/user problems and define the decision.

## Design
Translate friction hypotheses into testable experience changes.

## Engineering
Implement reliable instrumentation and treatments.

## Analytics
Own metric definitions, QA, analysis, experiment readouts, and decision evidence.

## Growth / Marketing
Use channel quality and economics—not traffic alone—to allocate spend.

## Finance / Leadership
Evaluate revenue, unit economics, risk, and investment implications.

## RevOps / Salesforce
Own CRM process integrity while Product Analytics measures workflow behavior.
