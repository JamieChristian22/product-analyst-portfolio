# Business Impact Sizing

## Frameworks
**Funnel Recovery:** Drop-off × recovery assumption × AOV.

**Revenue Scenario:** Baseline revenue × modeled improvement.

**Unit Economics:** CAC, gross-margin LTV, LTV:CAC, payback, ROI.

**Internal Workflow:** time saved, progression improvement, risk reduction, forecast credibility.

## Governance
Opportunity models are planning tools—not forecasts. Assumptions must be visible, sensitivity-tested, and separated from observed facts.
