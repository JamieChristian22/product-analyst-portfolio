# Portfolio Product Metric Tree

## North-Star Business Outcomes
Revenue • Retained Customers • Healthy Unit Economics • Workflow Productivity

## Product Outcome Metrics
Purchase Conversion • Activation • Retention • Time to Value • Stage Progression

## Diagnostic Metrics
View→Cart • Cart→Checkout • Checkout→Purchase • Feature Adoption • Journey Duration • Stale Rate • Overdue Rate

## Business Metrics
Revenue • AOV • ARPPU • CAC • LTV:CAC • Payback • ROI • Weighted Pipeline

## Guardrails
Performance • AOV • Refund/Cancellation • Support Burden • Alert Fatigue • Data Completeness

Metric trees prevent teams from optimizing a local metric while damaging the broader product or business.
