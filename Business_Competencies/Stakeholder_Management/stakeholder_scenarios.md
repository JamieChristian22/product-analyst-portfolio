# Stakeholder Scenarios

## PM wants to ship before instrumentation is ready
**Response:** define minimum viable instrumentation and delay causal measurement claims until event QA passes.

## Executive wants one metric to prove success
**Response:** provide a primary metric plus guardrails so local optimization does not hide customer or financial harm.

## Engineering says tracking is too expensive
**Response:** rank events by decision value; instrument only what is required to distinguish success, failure, and major root causes.

## Growth wants to scale a high-volume channel
**Response:** compare conversion quality, retention, CAC/payback, and downstream value—not traffic volume alone.

## Teams disagree on KPI definition
**Response:** establish one governed metric definition with grain, denominator, exclusions, source, and owner before analysis continues.
