# Product Analyst Operating Model

1. Frame the business objective.
2. Define the user/product problem.
3. Identify stakeholders and decision owner.
4. Translate the question into governed metrics.
5. Establish baseline and instrumentation quality.
6. Analyze behavior and diagnose friction.
7. Quantify opportunity and uncertainty.
8. Prioritize using evidence, strategy, and effort.
9. Define requirements and measurement plan.
10. Partner through delivery and QA.
11. Evaluate launch/experiment results.
12. Communicate recommendation and tradeoffs.
13. Monitor durability and update the decision log.

This operating model demonstrates the business competency expected around technical Product Analytics work.
