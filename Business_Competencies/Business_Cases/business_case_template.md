# Product Business Case

## Opportunity
Describe the user problem and measurable business consequence.

## Evidence
Baseline metrics, segments, qualitative evidence, and uncertainty.

## Options
1. Do nothing
2. Low-cost workflow/product change
3. Larger product investment

## Economics
Estimate implementation effort, opportunity size, incremental value, downside risk, and time-to-impact. Label assumptions.

## Recommendation
Select the option with the strongest combination of user value, strategic alignment, evidence, and economics.

## Validation
Define experiment or staged rollout, primary metric, guardrails, and decision threshold.

## Decision
Approve / test / defer / reject.
