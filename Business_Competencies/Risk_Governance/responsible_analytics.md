# Responsible Product Analytics

- Collect only data necessary for a defined product/business purpose.
- Avoid unnecessary sensitive attributes.
- Use aggregated reporting where individual-level detail is not needed.
- Check whether optimization could systematically disadvantage a user group.
- Do not present heuristics as predictive AI.
- Do not present correlation as causation.
- Label synthetic data, modeled assumptions, and scenario outputs.
- Define retention/access controls with the appropriate organizational owners.
- Escalate ambiguous privacy or compliance questions rather than guessing.
