# RAID & Assumption Register

## Risks
- Metric gaming
- Instrumentation gaps
- Sample-ratio mismatch
- Alert fatigue
- Performance regression
- Misinterpreted correlation
- Selection bias

## Assumptions
- Event taxonomy represents intended user behavior.
- Revenue/opportunity models are scenarios unless observed.
- Segment differences are not causal by themselves.

## Issues
Escalate broken tracking, definition conflicts, missing identifiers, and data freshness failures.

## Dependencies
Engineering instrumentation, Product decisions, Design treatment, Finance assumptions, Growth channel metadata.

## Governance Rule
Document the assumption before using it in a recommendation. Update the decision if the assumption fails.
