# User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

## Story 1 — Guided Next Action
As a user, I want a clear next action so I can progress without searching for what to do next.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Next action is visible at the decision point.
- Click/completion is instrumented.
- Error states are logged.
- Experience works across supported devices.

## Story 2 — Product Analyst Measurement
As a Product Analyst, I need trustworthy exposure and outcome events so I can evaluate whether the change improved the target behavior.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Unique experiment exposure event.
- Stable user/session identifier.
- Variant captured.
- Outcome timestamp follows exposure.
- Duplicate-event QA defined.
- Guardrail metrics available.

## Story 3 — PM Readout
As a PM, I need a concise experiment readout so I can make a ship, iterate, or stop decision.

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Effect size and uncertainty shown.
- Guardrails included.
- Segment results labeled exploratory unless pre-specified.
- Recommendation and limitations documented.
