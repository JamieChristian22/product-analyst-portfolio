# 🧠 Portfolio Mission

The analytical side of the portfolio answers:

> **What is happening, why does it matter, and what should Product do?**

This folder answers the next question:

> **How do we actually turn that recommendation into coordinated execution?**

The operating model is:

**🎯 Strategy → 🤝 Alignment → 📝 Requirements → ⭐ Prioritization → ⚙️ Delivery → 📊 Measurement → ✅ Decision**

# 🎯 Product Strategy

![Strategy](https://img.shields.io/badge/COMPETENCY-PRODUCT_STRATEGY-9333EA?style=for-the-badge)

Product strategy connects company goals to customer and product outcomes.

A strong strategy should define:

* Business objective
* User problem
* Product opportunity
* Target segment
* Desired outcome
* Success metrics
* Constraints
* Risks
* Strategic tradeoffs

```text id="x7z2qf"
Business Goal
│
├── Product Objective
│   ├── Product Outcome
│   │   ├── User Behavior
│   │   └── KPI
│   │
```


# 🎯 Strategic Fit Framework

Before recommending work, evaluate:

### 👤 Customer Value

Does this solve a meaningful user problem?

### 💰 Business Value

Does it support revenue, retention, efficiency, or risk reduction?

### 📊 Evidence

Is there enough data to justify action?

### 🧭 Strategic Alignment

Does it support the product or company direction?

### ⚙️ Feasibility

Can the team realistically execute it?

### ⏱️ Timing

Why now?

# 📝 Requirements Management

![Requirements](https://img.shields.io/badge/COMPETENCY-REQUIREMENTS_MANAGEMENT-059669?style=for-the-badge)

Insights become useful only when teams can translate them into implementable work.

A strong requirements package connects:

**User Need → Business Need → Product Requirement → Acceptance Criteria → Metric**

---

# 📄 Business Requirements

Business requirements describe **why** the initiative exists.

Examples:

* Reduce onboarding abandonment
* Improve first-purchase conversion
* Reduce stale Salesforce opportunities
* Improve paid-user retention

---

# ⚙️ Functional Requirements

Functional requirements describe **what the system must do**.

Example:

```text id="5jyfix"
The system shall notify a sales representative
when a high-value opportunity has had no recorded
activity for more than the defined stale threshold.
```

---

# 🛡️ Nonfunctional Requirements

Nonfunctional requirements describe **how well** the product must operate.

Examples:

* Performance
* Security
* Availability
* Accessibility
* Privacy
* Scalability
* Reliability

---

# 👤 User Stories

![Stories](https://img.shields.io/badge/DELIVERY-USER_STORIES-7C3AED?style=for-the-badge)

Standard format:

```text id="4yjry0"
As a [user],
I want [capability],
so that [business/user value].
```

Example:

```text id="8d35s3"
As a sales representative,
I want high-risk opportunities prioritized,
so that I can focus on the deals requiring
the most immediate attention.
```

---

# ✅ Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria define when a requirement is complete.

Example:

```text id="bi1aax"
Given an open opportunity with no activity
for more than 14 days,

When the user opens the priority queue,

Then the opportunity should display a stale-risk
indicator and recommended next action.
```

### Principle

> **A requirement without clear acceptance criteria is difficult to test and easy to misinterpret.**

---

# 🧮 RICE

```text id="5y2f8t"
RICE
=
(Reach × Impact × Confidence)
÷ Effort
```

Use when initiatives can be reasonably compared across:

* Reach
* Expected impact
* Evidence confidence
* Delivery effort

---

# 🟥 MoSCoW

### Must Have

Required for success.

### Should Have

High-value but not critical.

### Could Have

Useful if capacity permits.

### Won't Have — For Now

Explicitly outside current scope.

---

# ⚖️ Impact × Effort

|                 | Low Effort   | High Effort      |
| --------------- | ------------ | ---------------- |
| **High Impact** | 🚀 Quick Win | 🎯 Strategic Bet |
| **Low Impact**  | 🛠️ Fill-In  | ⏸️ Avoid / Defer |

---

* Dependencies
* Strategic fit

* Technical complexity
* Opportunity cost

# 📚 Backlog Management

A healthy backlog should contain work that is:

* Clearly described
* Prioritized
* Sized appropriately
* Linked to an outcome
* Supported by acceptance criteria
* Ready for discussion

### Principle

> **A backlog is not a parking lot. It is an ordered set of decisions.**

# ⚠️ Risk Management

![Risk](https://img.shields.io/badge/COMPETENCY-RISK_MANAGEMENT-DC2626?style=for-the-badge)

Every initiative contains uncertainty.

Risks may include:

### 🔧 Technical

Integration, scalability, architecture.

### 👤 User

Low adoption or poor usability.

### 📊 Data

Tracking gaps or poor metric quality.

### 💰 Financial

Weak economics or cost overruns.

### ⏱️ Delivery

Dependencies or timeline issues.

### ⚖️ Compliance

Privacy, legal, security, governance.

---

# 📋 RAID Framework

![RAID](https://img.shields.io/badge/FRAMEWORK-RAID-DC2626?style=for-the-badge)

Track:

### ⚠️ Risks

Potential future problems.

### ✅ Assumptions

Things believed to be true.

### 🚧 Issues

Problems already occurring.

### 🔗 Dependencies

Work relying on another team, system, or decision.

---

# 📐 Risk Scoring

```text id="slsd8c"
Risk Score
=
Probability × Impact
```

Use the score to prioritize mitigation, not to create false precision.

# 🔄 Change Management

Product requirements change.

The goal is not to eliminate change.

The goal is to manage it.

Evaluate:

### Why is the change needed?

### What requirement changes?

### What downstream work is affected?

### Does priority change?

### Does timeline change?

### Does measurement change?

### Who must approve?

# 📈 Post-Launch Measurement

Delivery is not finished when the feature ships.

Measure:

### Adoption

Are users using it?

### Behavior

Did behavior change?

### Outcome

Did the target KPI improve?

### Business Impact

Did the expected business value occur?

### Guardrails

Did anything get worse?

### Qualitative Feedback

What do customers and internal teams say?

---

# ✅ Post-Launch Decision

Potential outcomes:

### 🚀 Scale

The solution is working.

### 🔄 Iterate

Value exists but execution needs improvement.

### 🔍 Investigate

Results are unclear.

### 🛑 Roll Back / Stop

The solution is not creating sufficient value.
