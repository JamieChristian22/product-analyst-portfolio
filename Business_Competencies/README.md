# Business Competencies — Product Analyst Operating Model

**Product Strategy | Stakeholder Management | Requirements | Prioritization | Agile Delivery | Business Cases | Risk & Governance | Executive Communication**

## Purpose
This folder demonstrates how a Product Analyst operates inside a real cross-functional product organization.

The technical portfolio answers **Can I analyze the data?**
Analysis & Insights answers **What does the evidence mean and what should we do?**
Business Competencies answers **Can I work with Product, Design, Engineering, Finance, Growth, RevOps, and leadership to turn the decision into execution?**

## Operating Model
**Business Objective → User Problem → Stakeholder Alignment → Requirements → KPI/OKR → Prioritization → Delivery → Measurement → Risk Review → Executive Decision**

## Competencies Demonstrated
- Product strategy and business-model alignment
- KPI and OKR translation
- Stakeholder mapping and RACI
- Product requirements and acceptance criteria
- RICE and impact/effort prioritization
- Tradeoff analysis
- Agile/Scrum analytics partnership
- Release measurement and post-launch monitoring
- Business cases and ROI thinking
- Risk, assumption, issue, and dependency governance
- Executive communication
- Ethical and responsible analytics

## Product Analyst Positioning
These artifacts are intentionally Product Analyst-specific rather than a duplicate Business Analyst portfolio. The focus is on using business context, product metrics, evidence, and stakeholder alignment to improve product decisions and outcomes.
