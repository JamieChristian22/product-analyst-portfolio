# Prioritization & Tradeoffs

## Frameworks
### RICE
Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.

### Impact / Effort
Use for fast directional portfolio decisions.

### Strategic Fit
Evaluate alignment with product objective, user problem, economics, and technical feasibility.

## Tradeoff Questions
- Does this solve a validated user problem?
- Is the affected population meaningful?
- What is the expected product/business impact?
- How confident is the evidence?
- What is the implementation and analytics cost?
- What opportunity is displaced?
- What could deteriorate if this metric improves?

## Rule
A lower-effort initiative with strong evidence and measurable user value may outrank a more impressive but speculative feature.
