# Executive Communication Playbook

## 60-Second Readout
**Decision:** what leadership needs to decide.  
**Evidence:** 2–3 facts that matter.  
**Implication:** why the facts matter now.  
**Recommendation:** what should happen next.  
**Risk:** the largest uncertainty.  
**Measurement:** how success will be judged.

## Writing Standard
Lead with the decision—not the methodology. Put technical detail in supporting material. Quantify impact when assumptions are defensible. State uncertainty clearly.
