# OKR & KPI Framework

## Objective
Improve product value realization and sustainable growth.

## Key Results
- Improve the selected product outcome versus a documented baseline.
- Reduce measurable workflow or journey friction.
- Maintain or improve critical guardrails.
- Establish trustworthy instrumentation for the target behavior.

## KPI Hierarchy
**North Star:** durable customer/business value  
**Product Outcomes:** activation, conversion, retention, time-to-value, workflow progression  
**Diagnostics:** funnel-stage conversion, feature adoption, journey duration, stale rate  
**Business:** revenue, AOV/ARPPU, CAC, LTV:CAC, payback, ROI  
**Guardrails:** reliability, support burden, refunds/cancellation, alert fatigue, data quality

## Governance
Every KPI requires an owner, definition, grain, denominator, refresh cadence, source, and decision use.
