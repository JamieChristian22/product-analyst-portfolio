# Product Strategy Alignment

## Business Objective
Create sustainable product growth by improving user value while maintaining healthy economics and operational feasibility.

## Product Analyst Responsibilities
1. Translate company objectives into measurable product outcomes.
2. Build KPI trees connecting user behavior to business results.
3. Identify gaps between current and target performance.
4. Quantify opportunity and uncertainty.
5. Recommend product bets with measurable success criteria.
6. Monitor whether shipped changes create durable impact.

## Strategy Cascade
**Company Goal → Product Objective → User Outcome → Product Metric → Diagnostic Metric → Initiative → Experiment → Business Result**

## Example
Company goal: profitable growth  
Product objective: improve qualified conversion  
User outcome: reach value with less friction  
Primary metric: purchase conversion  
Diagnostics: View→Cart, Checkout completion, journey duration  
Guardrails: AOV, refunds, performance, support contacts  
Business result: incremental gross-margin contribution

## Decision Rule
A high-volume metric movement is not automatically strategically valuable. Prioritize changes that improve user outcomes and contribute to the business objective without violating guardrails.
