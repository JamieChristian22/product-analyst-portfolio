# KPI & Metric Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Decision Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paid Conversion | Paid users / acquired users | Acquisition-to-monetization effectiveness |
| 30d Retention | Paid users retained at day 30 / paid users | Early customer quality |
| Revenue | Attributed observed revenue | Monetization |
| ARPPU | Revenue / paid users | Paid-user value proxy |
| CAC | Marketing spend / paid users | Acquisition efficiency |
| ROI | (Attributed revenue - spend) / spend | Channel economics |
| Time to Paid | Days from signup to paid conversion | Monetization speed |
| LTV:CAC Proxy | ARPPU / CAC | Directional unit-economics proxy |

ARPPU and LTV:CAC are explicitly proxies in this portfolio dataset; a production model would use recurring gross-margin-adjusted lifetime value.
