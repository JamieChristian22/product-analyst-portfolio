# Executive Investment Recommendation

## Decision
**Do not scale aggregate acquisition spend based on the observed-period ROI.**

### Scale
Only channels that clear the scenario unit-economics threshold after validation.

### Optimize / Test
Channels with promising retention and LTV:CAC but weak observed-period payback should receive controlled conversion/activation tests rather than unrestricted budget.

### Investigate
Channels near break-even require attribution, audience, offer, and retention diagnosis.

### Reduce / Pause
Channels with weak economics should lose incremental budget until the underlying issue is understood.

## Operating Rule
Budget decisions should use **conversion + retention + CAC + gross-margin-adjusted LTV + payback** together.

This converts the negative observed ROI into an actionable portfolio-management decision instead of treating it as a reporting anomaly.
