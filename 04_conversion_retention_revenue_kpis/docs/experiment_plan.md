# Experiment Plan — Improve Paid Conversion Without Damaging Retention

## Hypothesis
A clearer value/plan recommendation during activation will increase paid conversion while preserving 30-day retention quality.

## Primary Metric
Paid conversion rate.

## Secondary Metrics
Time to paid, plan mix, ARPPU.

## Guardrails
30-day retention, refunds/cancellations, support contacts, CAC.

## Decision Rule
Ship only if conversion improves materially and retention/unit economics remain healthy.
