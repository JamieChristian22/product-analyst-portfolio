# KPI Tree

## North-Star Business Outcome
**Efficient retained paid-user growth**

### Acquisition
Acquired users → channel mix → spend

### Conversion
Paid conversion → time to paid → plan mix

### Retention
30-day retention → retained paid users

### Monetization
Revenue → ARPPU → revenue by plan/channel

### Efficiency
CAC → ROI → LTV:CAC proxy

The KPI tree prevents local optimization. For example, a channel with high conversion can still be unattractive if CAC is excessive or retained-user quality is weak.
