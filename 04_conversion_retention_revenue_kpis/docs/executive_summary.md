# Executive Summary — Conversion, Retention & Revenue KPIs

## Decision Context
Connect acquisition quality to paid conversion, 30-day retention, revenue, CAC, and ROI so Product/Growth leaders can decide where to invest and what to improve.

## Executive KPIs
- Acquired users: **5,000**
- Paid users: **1,415**
- Paid conversion: **28.3%**
- 30-day paid retention: **63.7%**
- Revenue: **$87,434.66**
- ARPPU: **$61.79**
- Marketing spend: **$209,712.99**
- CAC: **$148.21**
- ROI: **-58.3%**
- Average time to paid: **4.23 days**

## Findings
- **Organic Search** has the strongest modeled channel ROI.
- **Referral** has the strongest 30-day paid retention.
- **Social** has the weakest modeled ROI and should be diagnosed before additional spend.
- Channel decisions should use conversion + retention + unit economics together rather than optimizing acquisition volume alone.

## Recommendation
Protect high-quality channels, diagnose weak ROI channels, and test activation/offer improvements where conversion is weak but retention quality is healthy.
