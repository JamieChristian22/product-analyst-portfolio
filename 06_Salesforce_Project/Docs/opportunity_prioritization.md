# Opportunity Prioritization
The priority queue combines value, probability, age, inactivity, overdue status, and modeled risk.

Recommended actions:
- EXECUTIVE REVIEW: highest urgency/risk/value.
- REP FOLLOW-UP: material deal requiring immediate next action.
- PROGRESS: healthy opportunity that should advance.
- NURTURE: lower urgency/value; maintain appropriate cadence.

The score is a transparent prioritization heuristic, not a machine-learning prediction.
