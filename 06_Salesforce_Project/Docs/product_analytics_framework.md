# Product Analytics Framework — Salesforce Sales Workflow

## Product Question
How can the internal Salesforce sales workflow product help users move qualified opportunities forward faster, reduce workflow friction, and improve forecast reliability?

## Product Users
- Sales representatives
- Sales managers
- Revenue Operations
- Finance / forecasting stakeholders

## Product Funnel
**Opportunity Created → Qualified → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won**

## Product Metrics
- Stage progression rate
- Time in stage
- Opportunity aging
- Stale-opportunity rate
- Overdue-close-date rate
- Next-action completion proxy
- Forecast-category progression
- Closed-won conversion
- Revenue per opportunity
- Weighted pipeline
- High-risk opportunity recovery

## Product Outcome
The project treats Salesforce not only as a CRM dataset, but as an internal workflow product whose usability, process design, alerts, automation, and prioritization can influence user behavior and business outcomes.
