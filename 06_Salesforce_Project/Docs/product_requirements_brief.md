# Product Requirements Brief — Salesforce Opportunity Health Workspace

## Objective
Help sales users identify the opportunities that need attention and take the correct next action with less manual searching.

## User Problem
Reps and managers can spend time scanning CRM records rather than acting on the most important opportunities. Stale activity, overdue close dates, and inconsistent forecast categories reduce workflow efficiency and forecast trust.

## MVP
- Opportunity health score
- Priority queue
- Stale and overdue indicators
- Recommended next-action field
- Manager escalation state
- Filter by owner, stage, amount, industry, and risk
- Weekly opportunity-health summary

## Success Metrics
Primary: stale-opportunity rate.
Secondary: stage progression, time in stage, overdue rate, priority-queue action completion, Closed Won conversion.
Guardrails: task burden, alert fatigue, data completeness.

## Rollout
Instrument → baseline → pilot → A/B test → analyze → staged rollout → monitor.
