# Product Stakeholder Readout

## Product
Own the opportunity-health workflow, prioritization experience, experiment design, and adoption metrics.

## Sales Users
Provide qualitative feedback on workflow friction, alert usefulness, and actionability.

## RevOps
Own stage definitions, probability governance, forecast categories, and CRM process integrity.

## Engineering / Salesforce Admin
Implement instrumentation, workflow automation, alerts, and permissions.

## Analytics
Define metrics, establish baselines, analyze funnels/cohorts, and measure experiments.

## Leadership
Use product and revenue outcomes together to decide whether workflow improvements should scale.
