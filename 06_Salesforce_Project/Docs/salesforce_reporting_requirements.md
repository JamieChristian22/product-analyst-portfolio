# Salesforce Reporting Requirements
Required reports:
1. Executive Pipeline Scorecard
2. Stage Funnel and Aging
3. Forecast Category Summary
4. Rep Performance
5. Industry and Lead Source Performance
6. Stale/Overdue Opportunity Queue
7. Pipeline Coverage
8. High-Value Risk Review

Refresh: daily operational; weekly executive review.
Access: role-based; revenue data should follow Salesforce sharing/security rules.
