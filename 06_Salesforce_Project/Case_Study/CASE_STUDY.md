## Findings
- **Riley Wilson** leads modeled closed-won revenue.
- **Morgan Davis** has the largest stale-opportunity count and needs pipeline hygiene attention.
- Weighted pipeline is materially below gross pipeline, reinforcing the need to distinguish booked potential from probability-adjusted forecast.
- Aging, inactivity, overdue close dates, and forecast category should be reviewed together—not as separate reports.

## Executive Decision
Prioritize pipeline hygiene and high-value at-risk opportunities before assuming additional top-of-funnel volume is the answer. Use forecast-category governance, stale-deal SLAs, and weekly risk reviews to improve forecast credibility.


# Stakeholder Readout
Sales Leadership: review coverage, commit, stale/overdue risk, and high-value opportunities.
Sales Operations: enforce stage/probability definitions and close-date hygiene.
Reps: maintain next actions and progress priority opportunities.
Finance: consume governed forecast views rather than gross pipeline.
Analytics: own KPI definitions, forecast QA, segmentation, and scenario analysis.
