# 🧠 Product Analytics Mission

This project investigates a fundamental e-commerce product question:

> **Where are users experiencing the most friction in the purchase journey, which segments are most affected, and which product improvements should be prioritized?**

The objective is not simply to calculate conversion rates.

The analytical workflow connects:

**👤 User Behavior → 📉 Funnel Loss → 🔍 Segment Diagnosis → 💡 Product Hypothesis → 🎯 Prioritization → 🧪 Experiment → 📈 Business Impact**

The funnel is analyzed as a sequence of meaningful product behaviors rather than isolated event totals.

This allows the analysis to answer:

* Where is the largest absolute user loss?
* Which transition has the weakest conversion?
* Which segments experience disproportionate friction?
* Which loss represents the largest addressable opportunity?
* Which intervention should Product test first?

# 🔎 Analytical Framework

The project follows six analytical layers.

## 1️⃣ Funnel Performance

![Layer](https://img.shields.io/badge/LAYER_01-FUNNEL_HEALTH-2563EB?style=flat-square)

Measure:

* Viewed users
* Add-to-cart users
* Checkout users
* Purchasers
* Stage conversion
* Stage drop-off
* Overall conversion

### Question

> **Where is the customer journey breaking down?**

---

## 2️⃣ Segment Diagnosis

![Layer](https://img.shields.io/badge/LAYER_02-SEGMENT_DIAGNOSIS-7C3AED?style=flat-square)

Analyze funnel behavior across:

📱 **Device**
🛍️ **Product Category**
🌎 **Country / Geography**

### Question

> **Is the funnel problem universal, or concentrated among particular users?**

---

## 3️⃣ Root-Cause Hypothesis

![Layer](https://img.shields.io/badge/LAYER_03-ROOT_CAUSE-F59E0B?style=flat-square)

Potential friction hypotheses include:

* Product-detail clarity
* CTA placement
* Shipping-cost uncertainty
* Returns-policy visibility
* Trust signals
* Mobile usability
* Page performance
* Product information quality

These are treated as **hypotheses requiring validation**, not facts proven by funnel data alone.

---

## 4️⃣ Opportunity Sizing

![Layer](https://img.shields.io/badge/LAYER_04-OPPORTUNITY_SIZING-059669?style=flat-square)

A simple planning framework:

**Stage Drop-Off × Recovery Assumption × AOV**

This converts funnel friction into a directional business opportunity.

### Important

> **Opportunity sizing is a planning scenario — not a guaranteed revenue forecast.**

---

## 5️⃣ Product Prioritization

![Layer](https://img.shields.io/badge/LAYER_05-PRIORITIZATION-DC2626?style=flat-square)

Prioritization considers:

**Reach × User Impact × Business Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort**

The highest-volume problem is not automatically the best product investment.

The decision should consider:

* Size of affected population
* Severity of friction
* Revenue exposure
* Confidence in diagnosis
* Implementation effort
* Experiment feasibility
* Strategic alignment

---

## 6️⃣ Experimentation

![Layer](https://img.shields.io/badge/LAYER_06-EXPERIMENTATION-9333EA?style=flat-square)

Observational analysis identifies the opportunity.

Experimentation determines whether the proposed product change actually caused improvement.

# 🎯 Primary Product Insight

![Insight](https://img.shields.io/badge/KEY_INSIGHT-VIEW_%E2%86%92_CART_FRICTION-DC2626?style=for-the-badge)

The analysis identifies the **View → Add-to-Cart transition** as the primary funnel opportunity.

That makes the Product Detail Page an important investigation point.

Potential contributors may include:

**CTA visibility • Product information • Trust • Shipping clarity • Returns clarity • Mobile UX • Page performance**

But funnel data alone cannot determine which mechanism caused the behavior.

Therefore:

> **Diagnose with analytics. Validate with experimentation.**

---

# 💡 Product Recommendation

## 🥇 Priority

Improve the product-detail decision experience before investing primarily in lower-funnel optimization.

Potential treatments:

### 🛒 CTA Clarity

Make the primary purchase action easier to identify and use.

### 🚚 Shipping Transparency

Surface shipping expectations earlier.

### ↩️ Returns Confidence

Make return information easier to understand before cart entry.

### ⭐ Trust Signals

Surface useful ratings, reviews, or confidence-building information.

### 📱 Mobile Experience

Evaluate CTA placement, page speed, content hierarchy, and interaction friction on smaller screens.

---

# 🧪 Experiment Design

![Experiment](https://img.shields.io/badge/EXPERIMENT-PDP_DECISION_SUPPORT-7C3AED?style=for-the-badge)

## Hypothesis

> Improving product-detail decision support will increase View → Add-to-Cart conversion by reducing uncertainty before users commit to the cart.

### 🅰️ Control

Current product-detail experience.

### 🅱️ Treatment

Enhanced product-detail experience with improved decision support.

Potential components:

* More prominent CTA
* Shipping clarity
* Returns information
* Trust signals
* Improved mobile hierarchy

---

# 📏 Experiment Measurement

### 🎯 Primary Metric

**View → Add-to-Cart Conversion Rate**

### 📊 Secondary Metrics

* Overall Purchase Conversion
* Cart → Checkout
* Checkout → Purchase
* Revenue per Session

### 🛡️ Guardrails

* Average Order Value
* Page performance
* Error rate
* Refund / cancellation indicators
* Support contacts

### 🚨 Decision Rule

Do not ship simply because Add-to-Cart increases.

The treatment should improve the intended product behavior **without materially damaging downstream conversion, customer quality, or business outcomes.**

# 💰 Revenue Opportunity Model

![Revenue](https://img.shields.io/badge/MODEL-FUNNEL_RECOVERY_%E2%86%92_REVENUE-F59E0B?style=for-the-badge)

The project connects behavioral improvement to financial opportunity.

### Framework

```text
Incremental Users
=
Stage Drop-Off × Recovery Assumption

Incremental Revenue Opportunity
=
Incremental Users × Average Order Value
```

### Example Scenarios

**Conservative** → 5% recovery
**Target** → 10% recovery
**Stretch** → 15% recovery

These scenarios help Product and leadership understand the approximate economic magnitude of solving the problem.

They are **not forecasts of guaranteed results**.
