# Stakeholder Readout

## One-line takeaway
The largest growth opportunity is **before cart creation**, so the product team should prioritize PDP/mobile conversion work before investing in a checkout redesign.

## What changed my recommendation?
The funnel loses **3,079 sessions** between View and Add to Cart, materially more than later-stage losses.

## Who should act?
- **Product Manager:** prioritize PDP experiment in roadmap.
- **Product Designer:** design treatment variants.
- **Engineering:** instrument experiment and performance guardrails.
- **Product Analyst:** define exposure logic, QA event tracking, monitor experiment.
- **Growth/Marketing:** avoid scaling acquisition into low-converting experiences until funnel friction is addressed.

## Recommended sequence
1. Instrument and validate funnel events.
2. Launch mobile PDP A/B test.
3. Analyze conversion lift and guardrails.
4. If successful, roll out and monitor revenue/session.
5. Then evaluate category-specific personalization and checkout optimization.

## Risks / Limitations
- Dataset is simulated and does not include traffic source, customer tenure, page latency, promotion exposure, inventory, or shipping cost.
- Observed segment gaps are diagnostic, not causal.
- Revenue opportunity estimates assume current downstream conversion and AOV remain stable.
